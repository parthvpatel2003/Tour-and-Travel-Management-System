Pack Light

Take half the clothes you think you will need. Write a list of essentials, then reduce it and pack only what is necessary.

Carry an Extra Bank Card

Keep an extra debit or credit card with you in case your main card is lost or stolen.

Travel Alone at Least Once

Solo travel helps build confidence, independence and teaches valuable life skills.

Always Use Maps

Don't hesitate to use Google Maps or ask locals for directions.

Carry a Lock

A small combination lock helps keep your luggage safe while travelling.

Ask Hotel Staff

Hotel staff often know the best restaurants, attractions and local transport options.

Compare Flight Prices

Compare prices on different websites before booking your flight.

Carry Your ID

Always keep a government-issued ID with you for identification.

Carry a First Aid Kit

Keep basic medicines and a first-aid kit with you for emergencies.
